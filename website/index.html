06 / QUESTIONS

The important details.

Know the limits before you rely on a finding.

Does Open Forensic decide whether a bank made an error?+

No. It flags potential discrepancies under the rules you configure. Verify value dates, tariffs, contracts, exemptions, and relevant law before drawing a conclusion.

What statements can I import?+

CSV, XLSX, and standard MT940 files. Scanned PDFs and bank-specific PDF layouts are not supported in this build.

Where is my data stored?+

Transactions, review state, and saved PDFs are kept in this browser using IndexedDB. They are not encrypted at the application level. Clearing browser data removes the local workspace and saved documents.

Will an AI agent send documents for me?+

The assistant can prepare a filled PDF and save it in your Open Forensic workspace after you review it. Sending it to a bank or other third-party portal is not connected.

Can I sign in with ChatGPT?+

The desktop app includes an experimental ChatGPT sign-in preview for eligible accounts and an OpenAI API-key option. AI can help map columns, explain findings, and refine a draft after you choose to share the relevant data. The browser workspace works locally without AI sign-in when deployed.
